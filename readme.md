# Chapter 2: Related Work

## General Smart Cooking Systems
- Chen et al. introduce a smart kitchen designed for nutrition-aware cooking, leveraging pervasive computing to offer real-time feedback on the nutritional content of meals during preparation.
- Chi et al. focus on enabling calorie-aware cooking in a smart kitchen environment, utilizing sensors to track calorie intake from ingredients and providing users with real-time feedback to support healthier meal preparation.
- Kansaksiri et al. present "Smart Cuisine," which uses generative recipes and ChatGPT-powered nutrition assistance to promote sustainable cooking practices.
- Hakguder et al. propose a system for smart diet management through the analysis of food images and cooking recipes.
- Wolfson et al. examine the relationship between cooking at home, fast food consumption, meat consumption, and dietary carbon footprint among US adults, providing insights into food choices and their environmental impact.

## Cooking Support and Assistance
- Hashimoto et al. propose a smart kitchen as a user-centric cooking support system, aiming to assist users throughout the cooking process.
- Lim et al. present "Smart cook," a system employing multimodal learning to simplify cooking by recommending recipes based on available ingredients and potentially assisting with task execution.
- Mahajan and Ding introduce "Cueing Kitchen," a smart cooking assistant designed to provide guidance and support during the cooking process.
- Neumann et al. describe "kognichef," a cognitive cooking assistant designed to aid users in various aspects of cooking.
- Aoyama et al. propose a smart cooking support system that utilizes an interaction reproducing model to assist users during cooking.
- Ruiz et al. detail the design of a multisensor system intended for a smart cooking assistant, focusing on enhanced sensing capabilities.
- Weber et al. explore the design of interaction concepts for assisted cooking in smart kitchens, emphasizing human agency, proactivity, and multimodality.
- Majil et al. present an augmented reality-based interactive cooking guide to assist users during the cooking process.
- Doman et al. introduce "Smart VideoCooKing," a multimedia cooking recipe browsing application designed for portable devices.

## Smart Kitchen and IoT Platforms
- da Silva Medeiros et al. present Smartgas, an intelligent platform for monitoring cooking gas levels using IoT technologies and a mobile application.
- Sivakumar et al. discuss an Internet of Things-based smart cooking system, highlighting the integration of IoT for enhanced cooking experiences.
- Scheible et al. introduce SMARTKITCHEN, a media-enhanced cooking environment that likely integrates various smart technologies.
- Syamsudduha et al. explore the design of a future smart cooking machine system, outlining potential advancements in automated cooking.
- Sandeep and Nandini discuss the implementation of an IoT-based smart cooking environment.
- Xiaoguang et al. detail the design and implementation of a smart cooking system based on Amazon Echo.

## Automation and Digital Twins in Cooking
- Güngör and Güngör examine the use of smart cooking systems and automation within industrial kitchens in the gastronomy sector.
- Bautista et al. review automated cooking systems, discussing their benefits, challenges, and future directions.
- Svosve and Gudukeya detail the design of a smart electric cooking stove, focusing on its features and functionalities.
- Li et al. introduce the concept of an intelligent oven within a smart home environment.
- Cabeza-Gil et al. explore the use of digital twins for monitoring and predicting the cooking process of food products, using a French crêpe as a case study.

## Assistive Technologies for Cooking
- Bouchard et al. present a smart range designed to assist cognitively impaired individuals in cooking, aiming to enhance their independence and safety in the kitchen.
- Gagnon-Roy et al. conduct a user experience study of smart assistive technology for cooking, specifically for people with cognitive impairments following traumatic brain injury.
- Kosch et al. present "The Digital Cooking Coach," which uses visual and auditory in-situ instructions to assist cognitively impaired individuals during cooking.

## Safety and Monitoring in Cooking
- Ngankam et al. focus on real-time multiple object tracking for ensuring safety during cooking activities.
- Yared and Abdulrazak propose a sensor-based smart oven system to enhance cooking safety.
- Yared and Abdulrazak discuss the development of a context-aware smart oven to prevent cooking risks in the kitchens of elderly people.

## Specific Technologies and User Perception
- Talens et al. investigate consumer perception of smart cooking devices in the EU, focusing on designing a healthy and personalized breakfast.
- Voit et al. explore the use of different modalities for smart home notifications in a cooking scenario, presented as "The Attention Kitchen."
- Bono-Nuez et al. present the use of an inductor as a smart sensor for material identification in domestic induction cooking.

## Foundational Machine Learning Concepts
- Van der Maaten and Hinton introduce t-SNE, a dimensionality reduction technique for visualizing high-dimensional data.
- Vaswani et al. present the Transformer architecture and the concept of attention mechanisms, which have become fundamental in deep learning.

## Cooking Emissions and Environmental Impact
- Buonanno et al. investigate particle emission factors during various cooking activities.
- Cheng et al. characterize volatile organic compounds emitted from different cooking processes.
- Peng et al. examine the effects of cooking method, oil type, and food type on aldehyde emissions in cooking oil fumes.
- Wang et al. study the emissions of volatile organic compounds from cooking in Shanghai, with broader implications for China.
- Fameli et al. provide an inventory of commercial cooking activities and their emissions in a typical urban area in Greece.
